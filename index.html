3 · Travel & set-up

Find the job location, and the app picks the closest office by driving distance (Fort Frances, Kenora, Dryden, Sioux Lookout). Round trip is always charged; one-way trips of 10 km or less are free.

    Tip: in Google Maps, right-click the exact house and click the coordinates at the top of the menu to copy them. Short links (maps.app.goo.gl) cannot be read — open the link first and copy from the address bar.

    Drag the pin to the exact house. The distance recalculates when you drop it.

    Charged amounts editable override

    Vehicle = km × $0.98. Hours are rounded up to the nearest quarter hour. Type over these boxes to enter km and hours manually.

    An additional fixed "All Job Set Up Fee" of $140.00 is always included (as on the sheet).
